You are an expert clinical geneticist reviewing literature for evidence of pathogenic heterozygous variants in the gene {gene_name}.

Your task: analyze the following articles (from PubMed and ASHG abstracts) and extract clinical evidence about patients carrying heterozygous mutations in {gene_name}.

ARTICLES:
{articles}

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. For each article that describes one or more patients with heterozygous variants in {gene_name}, extract the information below.
2. Skip articles that are reviews, meta-analyses, or do not describe individual patients/case series with variants in this gene.
3. Report a maximum of {max_papers} papers. If there are more than {max_papers} relevant papers, prioritize those that:
   - Report the most patients
   - Are NOT from large-scale cohort studies (e.g., DeNovoWEST, mupit, DDD study, GeneDx cohort papers) unless they provide detailed per-patient phenotyping
4. Stratify variants by annotation: LoF (nonsense, frameshift, splice-site), missense, synonymous, or other (CNV, in-frame indel, etc.)

For each relevant paper, output a JSON object with these fields:
- "citation": article title and PMID (e.g., "Smith et al. 2020 - Title (PMID: 12345678)") or "ASHG YYYY - Title" for ASHG abstracts
- "num_patients": number of patients described with variants in {gene_name} (integer, or null if unclear)
- "variants": list of objects, each with:
    - "description": variant notation if available (e.g., "c.1234A>G, p.Arg412Gly")
    - "type": one of "LoF", "missense", "synonymous", "other"
    - "phenotypes": list of phenotypes observed in patient(s) with this variant
- "diagnostic_reason": why this variant was considered diagnostic/pathogenic, if stated (e.g., "de novo, absent from gnomAD, predicted damaging by CADD")
- "center_country": name and/or country of the clinical facility, if available
- "cohort": if the patient is part of a known cohort, state which one: "UDN", "GREGoR", "DDD", "RUMC", "GEL", or null

Output ONLY a JSON array of objects. No commentary before or after.
If no relevant clinical evidence is found, output an empty array: []

FAM222B LITERATURE EVIDENCE REPORT: Neurodevelopmental disorder gene assessment (heterozygous/autosomal dominant)

1. GENE SUMMARY
FAM222B (C17orf63) encodes a protein with a conserved intrinsically disordered domain (DCD222) that is predicted to bind Nemo-like kinase (NLK) and may act as an NLK modulator (PMID: 41365991). Its best-supported disease association is cardiovascular laterality defects, not a neurodevelopmental disorder. The retrieved literature contains no evidence linking it to a neurodevelopmental phenotype.

2. CLINICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Independent reports: two studies, with different phenotypes.
  - Cardiovascular laterality defects (PMID: 42632841): trio exome sequencing of 16 case-parent trios plus an exome survey of 2,109 individuals with situs inversus totalis, heterotaxy, or isolated CHD found six FAM222B variants. The recurrent c.899G>A (p.Arg300His) arose de novo in two unrelated families. The other five variants are not described in the available summary.
  - Cerebral cavernous malformation (PMID: 27587990): a dominantly segregating frameshift variant was found in one CCM family that was negative for CCM1-3. The authors concluded FAM222B is not a likely CCM gene.
- Total patients: not precisely stated. At least 2 probands carry the recurrent de novo variant, about 6 variant-carrying families were identified in the laterality study, and one further family was reported in the CCM study. The CCM family's variant is a weak, probably non-causal observation.
- Most common phenotypes: situs inversus totalis, heterotaxy, and congenital heart defects. No neurodevelopmental phenotypes (intellectual disability, developmental delay, autism, epilepsy) were reported.
- Predominant variant type: missense (recurrent p.Arg300His). A single frameshift was reported in the CCM family.
- Known cohorts (UDN, GREGoR, DDD, RUMC, GEL): none identified. The center was probably in Germany, but this is not confirmed.
- Strength of clinical evidence for a neurodevelopmental disorder: NONE. For cardiovascular laterality defects it is LIMITED to MODERATE, based on a recurrent de novo missense variant in only two families.

3. MODEL ORGANISM EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Organism: zebrafish only. Mouse and other models were not reported.
- Laterality study (PMID: 42632841): the fam222ba/bb double knockout showed aberrant cardiac looping in larvae and enlarged atrium and ventricle in adults. Injecting human p.Arg300His mRNA into Tg(kdrl:EGFP) embryos perturbed cardiogenesis. Mendelian ratios, lethality, and sample sizes were not reported.
- CCM study (PMID: 27587990): TALEN-generated fam222bb/fam222ba double mutants were viable and showed no CCM-like or vascular phenotype.
- Neurological phenotypes: none reported in either study.
- Consistency with human phenotype: the cardiac looping findings are consistent with the human laterality and CHD phenotype. There is no neurodevelopmental correlate. The mutant mRNA injection suggests a possible gain-of-function or dominant-negative effect, but this is unresolved. The knockout phenotype does not clearly match a heterozygous missense disease mechanism.
- Strength of model organism evidence: LIMITED for cardiac laterality, NONE for neurodevelopmental disease.

4. BIOLOGICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Molecular function and pathways: AlphaFold3 modelling and molecular dynamics predict that DCD222 binds NLK through a reverse docking motif, with a stable interface and an unobstructed catalytic site (PMID: 41365991). This links FAM222B to NLK-regulated WNT, NOTCH, VEGF, mTOR, and TGF-β signalling. DCD222 is conserved from cyclostomes to humans, which indicates strong constraint. Structural modelling places Arg300 at the NLK recognition interface (PMID: 42632841). NLK is linked to left-right axis determination.
- Other findings: a yeast 2-hybrid screen showed interaction with the tubulin cytoskeleton and with STAMBP (PMID: 27587990). Fam222B siRNA altered miRNA expression in B16 melanoma cells (PMID: 40789939). FAM222B is overexpressed in hepatocellular carcinoma (PMID: 40950660).
- Expression: zebrafish whole-mount in situ hybridization was performed (PMID: 42632841), but the results are not detailed. No brain or neurodevelopmental expression data were provided.
- Limitations: the NLK interaction is largely computational and has not been experimentally validated in the available summaries. The p.Arg300His functional effect has not been biochemically defined.
- Strength of biological evidence: LIMITED to MODERATE for a role in laterality and cardiac development, and LIMITED for neurodevelopment (indirect, via NLK and tubulin).

5. OVERALL ASSESSMENT
- Overall evidence level for FAM222B as a heterozygous neurodevelopmental disorder gene: INSUFFICIENT.
- Key strengths:
  - A recurrent de novo missense variant in two unrelated families (PMID: 42632841).
  - Concordant zebrafish cardiac looping phenotypes.
  - A plausible NLK-based mechanism.
  - Strong evolutionary conservation of DCD222.
- Key gaps and limitations:
  - No patients with neurodevelopmental phenotypes.
  - Few reported cases, and the other five variants are uncharacterized.
  - No association with large neurodevelopmental cohorts.
  - No mammalian models.
  - The one prior frameshift association (CCM) was refuted (PMID: 27587990).
  - No validated functional assay for the missense variant.
- Conclusion: FAM222B has emerging but limited evidence as a dominant disease gene for cardiovascular laterality defects, driven by recurrent de novo p.Arg300His. There is currently no evidence supporting a neurodevelopmental disorder, and it should not be classified as a neurodevelopmental disease gene without new patient data showing neurodevelopmental phenotypes.

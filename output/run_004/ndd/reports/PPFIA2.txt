PPFIA2 LITERATURE EVIDENCE REPORT: NEURODEVELOPMENTAL DISORDER (AUTOSOMAL DOMINANT)

1. GENE SUMMARY
PPFIA2 encodes liprin-alpha-2, a member of the liprin-alpha family of scaffold proteins. These proteins are related to LAR-type receptor protein tyrosine phosphatases and are enriched at the synapse. The extracted evidence links the protein to synaptic integrity (PMID: 41981183). The evidence here does not describe its molecular function in detail.

2. CLINICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Independent reports: 3 were found.
  - A de novo variant series (PMID: 41044885).
  - A single Japanese ID/MCA subject with a CNV (PMID: 26740234).
  - A single autism case from a trio-WGS cohort (PMID: 41010044).
- Total patients: about 11 (9 + 1 + 1). The 9 in the series come from two index individuals plus seven from large cohorts, and overlap with the other two reports cannot be excluded.
- Phenotypes:
  - Neurodevelopmental disorder, with overlapping phenotypes in the 9-person series. The abstract does not give details (PMID: 41044885).
  - Intellectual disability with multiple congenital anomalies (PMID: 26740234).
  - Autism spectrum disorder (PMID: 41010044).
- Variant types:
  - The series reports heterozygous de novo variants, but the abstract does not specify their types.
  - The CNV is a pathogenic CNV involving PPFIA2, with coordinates and copy-number state not given.
  - The one fully described variant is a de novo missense, c.343G>A p.Glu115Lys. It is absent from gnomAD, has high in silico deleteriousness scores (0.935 and 0.998) and is classed as a Principal Diagnostic Variant (PMID: 41010044).
  - The predominant type cannot be determined from the available data. A missense or other mechanism is possible, and LoF is plausible given the CNV and gene constraint, but neither is established.
- Cohorts: none of UDN, GREGoR, DDD, RUMC or GEL are identified. Cohort information is not reported, apart from a Japanese ID/MCA screening cohort of 450 subjects (PMID: 26740234).
- Strength of clinical evidence: MODERATE-to-LIMITED. I rate it LIMITED to MODERATE, leaning toward MODERATE only if the 9-patient series is confirmed. The de novo series is the key supporting element, but the abstract lacks variant-level and phenotype detail. The CNV case and the single autism case are each weak in isolation.

3. MODEL ORGANISM EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Organisms studied: none in the provided evidence.
- Knockout phenotypes: none reported, so there is no information on lethality, neurological phenotype or Mendelian ratios.
- Consistency with human phenotype: cannot be assessed.
- Strength of model organism evidence: NONE.

4. BIOLOGICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Pathways and function:
  - PPFIA2 is linked to synaptic integrity. In 5XFAD mice, lactate treatment increased PPFIA2 protein along with other synaptic proteins (OPCML, STXBP3, SYT1, VGLUT2, VSNL1), in a sex-dependent manner (PMID: 41981183).
- Expression:
  - PPFIA2 is differentially expressed in fetal versus adult ocular tissue, confirmed by RT-qPCR (PMID: 23422819).
  - Whole-blood PPFIA2 mRNA was higher in 90 heavy cannabis users than in 100 controls (logFC 0.17). Expression correlated negatively with estimated intelligence (B=-22.9, p=0.002), including in non-users (B=-28.5, p=0.037) (PMID: 30879928). This is a tentative link to cognition, but it is a peripheral-blood finding.
  - Brain-specific developmental expression data were not provided.
- Other associations (non-specific to neurodevelopment):
  - Common-variant GWAS signals for high-grade myopia (rs17306116, P<9×10^-4) (PMID: 23422819).
  - Chromosome Y loss in brain and Alzheimer's disease (rs114241159 in ACSS3/PPFIA2, p=1.53×10^-7) (PMID: 32147245).
  - Healthy aging (rs10746192, p=1.85×10^-6) (ASHG 2009 - Identifying the Genetic Underpinnings Associated With Healthspan).
  - A splicing variant in a high-IOP exome study (ASHG 2017 - Whole exome sequencing reveals candidate variants for elevated intraocular pressure in the Beaver Dam Eye Study).
  - Prostate cancer, where knockdown reduced proliferation and migration in LNCaP and C4-2 cells (PMID: 41617436). These cancer findings are not relevant to neurodevelopmental disease.
- Strength of biological evidence: LIMITED. The data are suggestive of a synaptic and cognitive role but indirect. There are no functional studies of the patient variants.

5. OVERALL ASSESSMENT
- Overall evidence level: LIMITED (emerging).
- Key strengths:
  - Recurrent de novo variants across about 9 individuals with overlapping phenotypes, supported by gene constraint metrics (PMID: 41044885).
  - A well-characterized de novo missense variant in autism (PMID: 41010044).
  - An independent CNV case with ID/MCA (PMID: 26740234).
  - Synaptic-protein and cognition-related expression associations (PMIDs: 41981183, 30879928).
- Key gaps and limitations:
  - Variant-level and phenotype detail is missing for the main series.
  - There is no clear mutational mechanism (LoF versus missense).
  - The CNV is imprecisely characterized and may involve other genes.
  - There is no model organism data and no functional validation of variants.
  - Most biological evidence comes from GWAS and cancer studies unrelated to neurodevelopment.
  - Cohort overlap is uncertain.
- Conclusion: PPFIA2 is an emerging candidate gene for an autosomal dominant neurodevelopmental disorder. The evidence rests mainly on a recent de novo series of 9 individuals plus isolated ID/MCA and ASD cases. The clinical signal is promising, but the absence of model organism data, functional studies and detailed phenotyping keeps the overall level at LIMITED. Publication of full case-level data and functional work could raise it to MODERATE or higher.

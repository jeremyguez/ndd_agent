CASZ1 LITERATURE EVIDENCE REPORT: CASZ1 AND NEURODEVELOPMENTAL DISORDER (HETEROZYGOUS / AUTOSOMAL DOMINANT)

1. GENE SUMMARY
CASZ1 (castor zinc finger 1) encodes a zinc finger transcription factor located in the 1p36.22 region. It regulates cardiac morphogenesis, vascular assembly, and temporal cell-fate decisions through chromatin-modifying complexes (NuRD, polycomb). It is a candidate contributor to the 1p36 deletion syndrome phenotype, particularly congenital heart disease.

2. CLINICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Independent reports: Four sources mention CASZ1 in a clinical context. None is a CASZ1-specific case report or series, and none describes an intragenic CASZ1 variant in a neurodevelopmental disorder (NDD) patient.
  - A Tunisian patient with an 11.64 Mb interstitial 1p36 deletion spanning 155 genes, including CASZ1 (PMID: 41556146). Phenotypes were IUGR, neonatal epilepsy, craniofacial dysmorphism, congenital heart defect, and corpus callosum agenesis. The phenotype cannot be attributed to CASZ1 alone.
  - An ASHG 2019 abstract (ASHG 2019 - 1p36 deletions: One cytogenetic band, multiple genetic syndromes and disease genes) covering more than 200 individuals with 1p36 deletions. Deletion/phenotype mapping, together with pLI >0.8 and mouse data, implicates CASZ1 haploinsufficiency in congenital heart defects. No CASZ1-specific patient count was given.
  - A CNV meta-analysis in NDD/ASD cohorts (PMID: 38850022) that flagged CASZ1 as a dosage-sensitive candidate within the 1p36.33-p36.32 deletion (about 1 patient).
  - A noncoding de novo mutation study in ASD (PMID: 40894881) reporting at least 3 noncoding de novo variants (CADD ≥15) in CASZ1 in probands. This is only suggestive, and CASZ1 is not the paper's main finding.
- Approximate patients: about 1 individually described patient, plus an unspecified number in aggregate deletion cohorts (more than 200 with 1p36 deletions, but not CASZ1-specific).
- Common phenotypes: congenital heart defects, intellectual disability, seizures, ASD/NDD, hearing loss, and craniofacial anomalies. These are confounded by co-deleted genes (SKI, RERE, PRDM16, and others).
- Variant type: Large multigene deletions (CNVs) and noncoding de novo variants. No LoF or missense intragenic variants are reported in NDD.
- Cohorts: No UDN, GREGoR, DDD, RUMC, or GEL patients are identified. The ASD data likely derive from sibling-controlled ASD cohorts, but this is not specified.
- Strength of clinical evidence: LIMITED (bordering on none for a CASZ1-specific NDD phenotype).

3. MODEL ORGANISM EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Organisms: Mouse (full and conditional knockouts, knockdown) and Xenopus (knockdown).
- Key phenotypes:
  - Full Casz1 knockout mice show myocardial hypoplasia, ventricular septal defect, noncompaction, and reduced cardiomyocyte proliferation, with dysregulated contractile and ion channel genes (PMID: 25190801). Lethality and Mendelian ratios are not reported in the extracted data.
  - Endothelial-specific knockout impairs vascular growth and pulmonary endothelial differentiation (PMID: 41702400).
  - Conditional knockout in the cochlea causes inner hair cell transdifferentiation to outer hair cells, outer hair cell loss, and hearing impairment (PMID: 39883789). This is consistent with the sensorineural hearing loss seen in 1p36 deletion cohorts.
  - Casz1 knockdown in the retina reduces rod fate and promotes gliogenesis (PMID: 33594190).
  - Xenopus knockdown causes vascular assembly defects and compromised myocardial integrity (PMID: 23639441; PMID: 24993940).
- Consistency with humans: Good for cardiac disease, and plausible for hearing loss, consistent with the 1p36 deletion phenotype. Direct brain or behavioral phenotypes are lacking. The neural evidence is limited to retinal progenitor fate and cochlear cell identity. No heterozygous mouse phenotype is described.
- Strength of model organism evidence: MODERATE (strong for cardiac, limited for neurodevelopment).

4. BIOLOGICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Molecular function: CASZ1 is a zinc finger transcription factor that interacts with the NuRD complex (PMID: 33594190) and polycomb repressor complex (PMID: 30072429). It controls temporal identity transitions in neural progenitors and higher-order nuclear organization in rods (PMID: 30072429). It also regulates EGFL7/RhoA in vascular morphogenesis (PMID: 23639441) and MYOD/MYOG feed-forward muscle differentiation (PMID: 32060262).
- Expression: Expressed in the developing myocardium, in terminally differentiated cardiomyocytes and skeletal muscle (PMID: 24633745), in pulmonary endothelium (PMID: 41702400), and in retinal progenitors. Brain expression is not described in the supplied evidence.
- Human genetic and functional data: A heterozygous nonsense variant (p.K351X) in a family with dilated cardiomyopathy showed complete penetrance, loss of transcriptional activity, and cosegregation (PMID: 28099117). This supports haploinsufficiency as a mechanism, but for cardiac disease rather than NDD.
- Other roles: Tumor-suppressor or oncogenic roles in neuroblastoma (PMID: 36243768), T-ALL (PMID: 38058200), and lung cancer (PMID: 36777515). These are of limited relevance to NDD.
- Strength of biological evidence: MODERATE (strong for chromatin regulation and cardiac function, limited for neurodevelopment specifically).

5. OVERALL ASSESSMENT
- Overall evidence level: LIMITED (for CASZ1 as an autosomal dominant NDD gene).
- Strengths: Plausible haploinsufficiency mechanism (pLI >0.8, familial LoF in DCM). Consistent mouse cardiac and hearing phenotypes. Chromatin-regulatory roles in neural progenitor fate. CASZ1 sits within a recurrent NDD-associated CNV region.
- Gaps: No reported intragenic LoF or missense variants in individuals with NDD. All human NDD-related data come from large deletions with confounding genes or from non-specific noncoding variants. There are no brain-specific knockout phenotypes, and no Mendelian ratio or lethality data are provided. No established cohorts contribute cases.
- Conclusion: CASZ1 is a credible contributor to the cardiac and possibly other features of 1p36 deletion syndrome. Evidence for an isolated heterozygous CASZ1-related neurodevelopmental disorder is currently limited. Identification of de novo or LoF intragenic variants in individuals with NDD would be needed before gene-disease validity can be upgraded.

7. CASZ1 promotes migration, invasion, and metastasis of lung cancer cells by controlling expression of ITGAV (PMID: 36777515)

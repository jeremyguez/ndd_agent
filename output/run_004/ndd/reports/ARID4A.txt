ARID4A LITERATURE EVIDENCE REPORT: Heterozygous variants and neurodevelopmental disorder

1. GENE SUMMARY
ARID4A (also called RBBP1) encodes an ARID/Tudor/chromodomain-containing chromatin-associated protein. It is a retinoblastoma-binding transcriptional co-repressor/co-regulator that acts in histone-modifying and epigenetic complexes. It binds Rb-family proteins through an LxCxE motif (PMID: 35716663) and binds DNA and nucleosomes (PMID: 33675746; PMID: 40794873).

2. CLINICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Independent reports: Four were found. One is a conference abstract case series (ASHG 2017 - ARID4A de novo variants identified by exome sequencing among individuals with neurodevelopmental disorders). The other three are a schizophrenia family study (PMID: 35365808), a single developmental language disorder (DLD) case (PMID: 39948625), and a single ASD case in a large cohort (PMID: 39632905).
- Patients: About 9 or more individuals with variants. The ASHG 2017 series has 6 (from 11,649 NDD probands, GeneDx), the DLD report 1, the ASD report 1, and the schizophrenia report has several affected siblings in Han Chinese families (number not stated).
- Phenotypes: In the ASHG 2017 series, developmental delay and behavioral abnormalities were present in 6/6. Autism was present in 4/6, tone abnormalities in 4/6, and developmental regression in 3/6. Intellectual disability, brain anomalies and facial dysmorphism also occurred. The brain anomalies were thin corpus callosum, absent septum pellucidum, holoprosencephaly in one patient, and pontocerebellar atrophy in another. Facial dysmorphism was seen in 2 patients. The other reports describe isolated schizophrenia, severe DLD without ID/ASD/CAS, and ASD.
- Variant types: Missense variants predominate. The ASHG series has 5 missense (4 confirmed de novo, 1 possibly paternal mosaic) and 1 frameshift de novo. The other reports add p.His411Asp (PMID: 35365808), p.E564A de novo (PMID: 39632905) and a frameshift, p.(Glu514ArgfsTer3) (PMID: 39948625). Specific variant notations were not given for the ASHG cases. The ASHG series also mentions a prior 14q23 deletion that includes ARID4A, which suggests haploinsufficiency.
- Cohorts: No UDN, GREGoR, DDD, RUMC or GEL cohort was identified. The cases come from GeneDx, a Chinese family study, and ASD and DLD cohorts.
- Limitations: The main series is an abstract with no peer-reviewed detail. The DLD variant was only a candidate, not classified as pathogenic. The schizophrenia variant has a different phenotype and probable inheritance in families, and the number of cases is not stated.
- Strength of clinical evidence: LIMITED (leaning toward moderate if the ASHG series is published). There are recurrent de novo variants and a consistent NDD phenotype, but there is no peer-reviewed series, no recurrent specific variants and no detailed phenotyping.

3. MODEL ORGANISM EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Organisms: Mouse only.
- Key phenotypes: Arid4a-/- mice are viable (PMID: 18728284). They show ineffective hematopoiesis, then monocytosis, anemia, thrombocytopenia, bone marrow failure with myelofibrosis, a CMML-like disorder, and progression to AML (5/42 Arid4a-/- mice and 10/12 Arid4a-/-Arid4b+/- mice). Hoxb gene expression was decreased and H3K4, H3K9 and H4K20 trimethylation increased. Arid4a-/-Arid4b+/- mice have progressive male infertility, hypogonadism, spermatogenic arrest and a leaky blood-testis barrier (PMID: 23487765). Epigenetic and imprinting effects were also reported (PMID: 17043311).
- Neurological phenotypes and Mendelian ratios: Neither was reported in the provided data. No heterozygous Arid4a phenotype was described.
- Consistency with human phenotype: Low. The hematologic and reproductive phenotypes do not match the human NDD phenotype. The mouse data support an essential role in chromatin regulation but not a neurodevelopmental role.
- Strength of model organism evidence: LIMITED.

4. BIOLOGICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Pathways and function: ARID4A is a chromatin regulator and Rb-pathway co-repressor. It binds the Rb-family protein p107 through its LxCxE motif (PMID: 35716663). It has an N-terminal double Tudor DNA-binding domain (PMID: 33675746) and was identified as a nucleosome-binding protein in an AlphaFold screen of about 7,600 proteins (PMID: 40794873). It also acts in AR/RB transcriptional pathways (PMID: 23487765) and in histone-modification and imprinting regulation (PMID: 17043311).
- Expression: Brain or neurodevelopmental expression data were not provided. Expression is documented in Sertoli cells and bone marrow. One patient with ring chromosome 14 showed ARID4A downregulation in blood (ASHG 2014 - Position effects modify gene expression in a ring chromosome 14?).
- Key experimental findings: The p.His411Asp variant gives a less compact ARID domain, weaker dsDNA binding, reduced G0-G1 arrest and increased proliferation in HEK293T cells (PMID: 35365808). ARID4A acts as a tumor suppressor, and its knockdown increases proliferation in gastric cancer cells (PMID: 28486502) and prostate cancer cells (PMID: 29797600). These are cancer-oriented data, not neurodevelopmental.
- Strength of biological evidence: MODERATE for the molecular function of ARID4A and for a functional effect of one missense variant. It is LIMITED for neurodevelopmental relevance specifically.

5. OVERALL ASSESSMENT
- Overall evidence level: LIMITED
- Key strengths:
  - Multiple de novo variants (one frameshift, several missense) in a large trio exome cohort with a consistent phenotype of DD, behavioral abnormalities and autism.
  - Additional de novo ASD and candidate LoF DLD reports.
  - A plausible chromatin-regulator mechanism.
  - A functional impact shown for a missense variant.
  - Possible haploinsufficiency, supported by a 14q23 deletion and LoF intolerance.
- Key gaps:
  - The main series is an unpublished conference abstract with no variant-level details.
  - No recurrent variants or peer-reviewed cohort data.
  - No functional validation of the NDD-associated variants.
  - No neurological phenotype in mouse models.
  - No brain expression data in the provided evidence.
  - The schizophrenia, DLD and ASD reports are heterogeneous or low-confidence, and one possible case of paternal mosaicism has unclear inheritance.
- Conclusion: ARID4A is a plausible candidate NDD gene for autosomal dominant inheritance, based on about 6 de novo cases in the ASHG 2017 series and a few additional neuropsychiatric reports. The evidence remains limited because there are no peer-reviewed case series, no NDD-relevant functional or animal data, and no replicated cohorts. Further case reports with detailed phenotypes, functional testing of NDD variants and neurodevelopmental models are needed to establish the gene-disease relationship.

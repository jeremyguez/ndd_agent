KIAA0100 (BLTP2) – Literature Evidence Report for Heterozygous Neurodevelopmental Disorder Association

1. GENE SUMMARY
KIAA0100, now named BLTP2 (bridge-like lipid transfer protein 2), is a large gene at 17q11.2 encoding a bridge-like lipid transfer protein with FMP27 domains. It localizes to ER–plasma membrane (ER-PM) contacts and is proposed to mediate bulk ER-to-PM lipid transport and to regulate PM dynamics (PMID: 40899996; PMID: 39974967).

2. CLINICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Independent reports: One source, an aggregate statistical analysis, not an individual case report or clinical case series (PMID: 35468861). It is a large-scale unified analysis of single-nucleotide and copy number variants in 41,165 NDD cases, including DDD and denovo-db data.
- Patients: The number of KIAA0100 patients is not stated. The 41,165 is the size of the whole cohort, not the number of carriers. The text states only that there are recurrent de novo variants at the same amino acid positions.
- Phenotypes: Only an unspecified neurodevelopmental disorder is recorded. No detailed clinical features, such as intellectual disability, seizures, or dysmorphism, are available.
- Variant type: Predominantly de novo damaging missense (d-MIS) variants. The specific substitutions were not given, and no LoF variants are reported.
- Cohorts: The DDD cohort, with denovo-db data (PMID: 35468861). There is no evidence from UDN, GREGoR, RUMC or GEL.
- Inheritance: De novo, consistent with a heterozygous dominant mechanism, but there are no segregation or functional data on the variants.
- Strength of clinical evidence: LIMITED. The gene is one of 26 candidate genes (with PSMC3, PIP5K1C, SEPT2, KBTBD7, REST, MAST3) flagged by recurrence of de novo missense variants. This is statistical, without case-level phenotyping, and has not been independently replicated.

3. MODEL ORGANISM EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Organisms studied: None reported in the evidence provided.
- Key phenotypes: No knockout or knock-in data on neurological phenotypes, lethality, or Mendelian ratio deviations.
- Consistency with human phenotype: Cannot be assessed.
- Strength of model organism evidence: NONE

4. BIOLOGICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Pathways and function: BLTP2 is a bridge-like lipid transfer protein. It is recruited to ER-PM contacts through phosphoinositides, the adaptors FAM102A/FAM102B, and N-BAR domain proteins at membrane-connected tubules (PMID: 39974967). It tethers the ER to tubular endosomes once they are continuous with the PM, and to macropinosomes fusing with the PM, supporting a role in bulk ER-to-PM lipid transport. Loss of BLTP2 causes accumulation of intracellular vacuoles, many connected to the PM (PMID: 40899996). Bioinformatic analysis predicted a secretory pathway protein with a signal peptide, a transmembrane region and FMP27 domains (PMID: 29404013).
- Expression: Western blot in U937 cells suggested two protein products (254 kDa and <250 kDa), and Northern blot showed two mRNA species (PMID: 29404037). Most expression data come from cancer contexts, such as overexpression in breast carcinoma for the homologous BCOX1 (PMID: 16289875), high expression with 17q11.2 amplification in breast tumors (PMID: 37145128), and association with survival in lung squamous cell carcinoma (PMID: 39724502). No brain or developmental expression data are provided.
- Experimental findings: Knockdown of KIAA0100 reduced aggression, invasion and anoikis resistance in MDA-MB-231 cells, with suggested microtubule and HSPA1A interactions (PMID: 29867023). CRISPR/Cas9 knockdown in U937 cells inhibited proliferation and promoted apoptosis (PMID: 29404013). The circ_0000520/miR-512-5p/KIAA0100 axis promotes lung cancer malignancy (PMID: 35866672).
- Relevance to neurodevelopment: Indirect. Membrane lipid transport and PM dynamics are plausible in neurons, but no neuronal studies are cited, and most functional data are oncology-focused.
- Strength of biological evidence: LIMITED to MODERATE for molecular function, but LIMITED for relevance to NDD.

5. OVERALL ASSESSMENT
- Overall evidence level: LIMITED
- Key strengths: Recurrent de novo damaging missense variants at the same residues in a very large NDD cohort (PMID: 35468861), and a well-characterized cellular role in lipid transport at ER-PM contacts (PMID: 40899996; PMID: 39974967).
- Key gaps: No case-level clinical data, no carrier count, and no specified variants or phenotypes. There is no replication in independent cohorts, no LoF evidence, no functional testing of the variants, no model organism data, and no brain-specific expression or functional data. The biological literature is mostly cancer-related.
- Conclusion: KIAA0100 is a candidate NDD gene supported mainly by statistical enrichment of recurrent de novo missense variants in a large aggregate analysis. Its function as a lipid transfer protein gives some biological plausibility, but the lack of detailed clinical reports, variant-level functional data and animal models means the gene-disease relationship is not established. Further case reports with phenotyping, variant functional studies, and neuronal or animal models are needed.

LATS1 LITERATURE EVIDENCE REPORT: LATS1 AS A NEURODEVELOPMENTAL DISORDER GENE (HETEROZYGOUS / AUTOSOMAL DOMINANT)

1. GENE SUMMARY
LATS1 (large tumor suppressor kinase 1) is a serine/threonine kinase in the core Hippo pathway. Together with LATS2, it phosphorylates and inhibits the transcriptional co-activators YAP and TAZ, which controls cell proliferation, differentiation and tissue homeostasis. It is best characterized as a tumor suppressor.

2. CLINICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Independent reports: One, a single five-generation Chinese Han family (PMID: 35986120).
- Patients: About 9 affected individuals, all from that one family.
- Phenotype: Familial cerebral cavernous malformations (CCMs) on MRI. CCM1-3 variants were excluded. No neurodevelopmental phenotype (intellectual disability, developmental delay, autism, epilepsy as a primary feature) was described in the extracted data.
- Variant type: One heterozygous missense variant, c.821C>T, p.Thr274Ile. No loss-of-function variants were reported.
- Supporting data: WES after exclusion of CCM1-3, co-segregation by Sanger sequencing, damaging in silico predictions, and impaired angiogenesis in HUVEC assays (PMID: 35986120).
- Cohorts: None of the patients are from UDN, GREGoR, DDD, RUMC or GEL. The data come from a single Chinese center.
- Strength of clinical evidence: LIMITED for a vascular (CCM) phenotype, based on a single family. For a neurodevelopmental disorder, it is effectively NONE.

3. MODEL ORGANISM EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Organism: Mouse only. All studies used conditional knockouts, and all of them deleted Lats1 and Lats2 together (Lats1/2), so the specific contribution of Lats1 cannot be separated.
- Key phenotypes:
  - Adipocyte Lats1/2 loss caused dedifferentiation and adipose fibrosis (PMID: 36229481).
  - Lats1/2 inactivation in the sinoatrial node caused severe sinus node dysfunction, fibrosis and reduced Ryr2. Yap/Taz deletion rescued this (PMID: 36317529).
  - Lats1/2 loss in cardiac fibroblasts drove glycolysis-dependent fibroinflammation and fibrosis (PMID: 41165345).
  - Endothelial Lats1/2 deletion blocked tip cell migration during retinal angiogenesis, phenocopying Yap/Taz-deficient mutants (PMID: 28973878).
  - Intestinal Lats1/2 knockout caused crypt hyperproliferation (PMID: 33950519).
- Not reported: Lethality, Mendelian ratios, infertility and sample sizes. No neurological or neurodevelopmental phenotypes were reported, and no heterozygous or Lats1-only models were described.
- Consistency with human phenotype: Only partial, and only by inference. The endothelial and angiogenesis findings (PMID: 28973878) are loosely consistent with a vascular CCM phenotype. There is no consistency with a neurodevelopmental phenotype.
- Strength of model organism evidence: LIMITED for vascular relevance, and NONE for neurodevelopmental disease.

4. BIOLOGICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Pathways and function: LATS1/2 act downstream of several upstream signals.
  - GPCR signaling: G12/13-coupled receptors inhibit LATS1/2, while Gs-coupled receptors activate them (PMID: 22863277).
  - Semaphorin signaling: Class 3 Semaphorins activate LATS1/2 via PlexinA and inhibit angiogenesis (PMID: 35613278).
  - DDR1 phase separation: DDR1 co-condenses with LATS1 and blocks MOB1 binding, which inactivates LATS1 and activates YAP (PMID: 36475898).
  - RIPK4: RIPK4 is an alternative upstream kinase that phosphorylates LATS1/2 (PMID: 40570855).
- Non-canonical functions:
  - LATS1/2 phosphorylate STAT1 at Ser727 to support MHC-I expression (PMID: 38383447).
  - LATS1 regulates autophagy through Beclin-1, independently of its kinase activity (PMID: 32944618).
  - Lats1/2 sustain intestinal stem cells and Wnt signaling (PMID: 32259481).
- Disease associations: LATS1 expression is reduced in renal cell carcinoma (46.7% positive vs 76.7% in normal kidney) with promoter hypermethylation (PMID: 25270913). It is also reduced in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (11.3% vs 59.3% in normal epithelium) (PMID: 36029506). These findings support tumor suppressor activity.
- Brain expression and neurodevelopment: The extracted evidence contains no brain expression data and no neuronal or neurodevelopmental functional studies.
- Strength of biological evidence: MODERATE for the gene's general function in the Hippo pathway and in angiogenesis. It is LIMITED to NONE for neurodevelopmental relevance.

5. OVERALL ASSESSMENT
- Overall evidence level: INSUFFICIENT for LATS1 as an autosomal dominant neurodevelopmental disorder gene.
- Key strengths:
  - LATS1 has a well-characterized role in Hippo-YAP/TAZ signaling.
  - A single family shows co-segregation of a missense variant with CCMs, with supporting endothelial functional data (PMID: 35986120).
  - Mouse endothelial data show a role for Lats1/2 in angiogenesis (PMID: 28973878).
- Key gaps:
  - There is only one family, no de novo variants and no LoF variants.
  - The reported phenotype is vascular (CCM), not neurodevelopmental.
  - Mouse models are Lats1/2 double knockouts, with no Lats1-specific or heterozygous models and no neurological phenotyping.
  - There are no brain expression or neuronal function data.
  - There are no cohort-level data (UDN, DDD, GREGoR and others), and no independent replication.
- Conclusion: The available evidence does not support LATS1 as a neurodevelopmental disorder gene. The single reported association is with familial cerebral cavernous malformation, based on one missense variant in one family (PMID: 35986120). Further families with LATS1 variants, ideally with neurodevelopmental phenotypes, plus Lats1-specific animal models, would be needed to establish any Mendelian disease association.

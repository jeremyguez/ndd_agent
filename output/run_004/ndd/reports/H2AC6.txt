H2AC6 NEURODEVELOPMENTAL DISORDER GENE EVIDENCE REPORT

1. GENE SUMMARY
H2AC6 (H2A clustered histone 6; formerly HIST1H2AE) encodes a replication-dependent core histone H2A variant. Together with H2B, H3 and H4 it forms the nucleosome octamer, so it contributes to chromatin packaging and the regulation of gene expression.

2. CLINICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Independent case reports or case series: none. The only clinical-level evidence is one statistical, cohort-level observation from a large targeted sequencing study of NDD/autism trios (PMID: 33004838).
- Total patients: not specified. The study reports an excess of de novo missense variants in H2AC6 (dnMIS q = 0.053, FDR 5%), but no individual variants, patient counts or per-patient phenotypes were available. The gene was also selected as a CNV candidate in that study.
- Phenotypes: only the general label "neurodevelopmental disorder/autism cohort". No specific phenotypes are reported.
- Variant type: de novo missense, as a group signal. Individual variants, their positions and their predicted functional effects are not described. There is no evidence for loss-of-function variants.
- Known cohorts (UDN, GREGoR, DDD, RUMC, GEL): none identified. The source is a targeted-sequencing NDD trio cohort, and the center and country are not specified.
- Strength of clinical evidence: LIMITED. The signal is a single statistical enrichment that only barely passes a 5% FDR threshold. It has no phenotypic characterization, no replication and no segregation or functional data.

3. MODEL ORGANISM EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Organisms studied: none found.
- Knockout phenotypes: no data on neurological phenotypes, lethality or Mendelian ratios. Redundancy among the many clustered histone H2A genes could complicate single-gene knockout interpretation.
- Consistency with human phenotype: cannot be assessed.
- Strength of model organism evidence: NONE

4. BIOLOGICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Pathways and function: H2AC6 is a core histone gene involved in nucleosome and chromatin organization. A colon adenocarcinoma gene set that included it was enriched for protein heterodimerization and neutrophil extracellular trap formation (PMID: 42176930).
- Expression: no brain or neurodevelopmental expression data were provided. The available expression data come from non-neural or disease contexts:
  - H2AC6 was upregulated in degenerated intervertebral discs and was one of five core PANoptosis-related genes. Baicalin downregulated it (PMID: 40463856).
  - It was a differentially expressed gene in esophageal squamous cell carcinoma and was linked to survival (PMID: 37641095).
  - It was a hub prognostic gene in colon adenocarcinoma, with a 3-year ROC AUC of 0.665 in a TCGA-COAD cohort of 524 samples (PMID: 42176930).
  - Its transcript was downregulated in serum extracellular vesicles after exercise in 14 adults with long COVID (q < 0.05) (PMID: 41588020).
- Other experimental findings:
  - In AngII-induced glomerular endothelial injury, miR-155-5p negatively regulated H2AC6 (PMID: 34926670).
  - SMR analysis linked higher genetically predicted H2AC6 expression to intervertebral disc degeneration risk (p_SMR < 0.05, p_HEIDI > 0.01) (PMID: 40988222).
- None of these studies address neurodevelopment, the effect of missense variants on histone function, or a mechanism connecting H2AC6 to NDD.
- Strength of biological evidence for an NDD role: LIMITED. The studies show that the gene is expressed and dysregulated in various diseases, but they are not relevant to NDD.

5. OVERALL ASSESSMENT
- Overall evidence level: LIMITED (bordering on INSUFFICIENT) for H2AC6 as an autosomal dominant NDD gene.
- Key strengths: a statistically suggestive de novo missense enrichment in a large NDD trio cohort (PMID: 33004838). Biologically, H2AC6 is a plausible candidate because it encodes a core histone, and other histone and chromatin genes are established NDD genes.
- Key gaps:
  - There are no individual patient reports, variant details, phenotypes or segregation data.
  - The statistical signal is marginal (q = 0.053) and has not been replicated.
  - There are no model organism data.
  - There are no functional studies of the variants.
  - Existing biological data come from oncology, degenerative disease and other non-neurological settings.
  - H2A cluster genes are highly similar and may be hard to sequence and call reliably.
- Conclusion: H2AC6 is a candidate NDD gene supported only by a single marginal statistical enrichment of de novo missense variants (PMID: 33004838). Without detailed clinical cases, replication, functional validation or model organism data, the evidence is insufficient to establish a gene-disease relationship. Further work should look for additional de novo variants in large NDD cohorts, characterize the phenotypes, and test the functional effects of the variants.

SEC23A LITERATURE EVIDENCE REPORT: CHD and CDH (heterozygous / autosomal dominant framing)

1. GENE SUMMARY
SEC23A encodes a core subunit of the COPII coat. Together with SEC24 it forms the inner coat that selects cargo and drives ER-to-Golgi vesicle formation, notably for bulky cargo such as procollagen. Its best-established human disease link is autosomal recessive cranio-lenticulo-sutural dysplasia (CLSD), not CHD or CDH.

2. CLINICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
The clinical evidence array contained no entries for either condition, so no case reports, case series, or cohort data (UDN, GREGoR, DDD, RUMC, GEL) were found. The number of patients is 0, no variant type predominates, and no phenotypes were observed.

The only human data come from the biological evidence. An ASHG 2010 abstract (ASHG 2010 - A novel SEC23A missense mutation causing Cranio-Lenticulo-Sutural Dysplasia is involved in the MAPK signaling pathway) describes one CLSD patient with the missense variant M702V. This is a skeletal/craniofacial disorder and was not reported with CHD or CDH. That abstract does not support a heterozygous dominant CHD or CDH mechanism.

Strength of clinical evidence for congenital heart disease (CHD): NONE
Strength of clinical evidence for congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH): NONE

3. MODEL ORGANISM EVIDENCE SUMMARY
Organisms studied are zebrafish and mouse.
- Zebrafish crusher mutant (sec23a L402X nonsense): malformed craniofacial skeleton, kinked pectoral fins, short body length, distended ER in chondrocytes, and severely reduced cartilage ECM and type II collagen (PMID: 16980978). The sec23a morpholino reproduces a CLSD-like phenotype (PMID: 16980979). Rescue with wild-type SEC23A but not O-GlcNAc site mutants was shown in the CLSD model (PMID: 29161034).
- Zebrafish sec23a mutants show failed lymphatic endothelial cell migration because Col2a1 secretion from the notochord sheath fails (PMID: 32839180). This is a vascular/lymphatic defect, not a cardiac malformation.
- The creb3l2 hypomorph (feelgood), which acts upstream of sec23a/b and sec24d, shows a malformed head skeleton and a collagen backlog in the ER (PMID: 21729877).
- Mouse: Bbf2h7-/- mice, which lack the upstream regulator of Sec23a, die perinatally from an immature chest cavity and show chondrodysplasia. Sec23a reintroduction restored matrix secretion (PMID: 19767744). This is an indirect model and not a Sec23a knockout. The chest cavity defect is skeletal and was not described as diaphragmatic hernia.
- SEC23A and SEC23B are functionally interchangeable in vivo (PMID: 30065114).

No model reports a cardiac malformation or diaphragmatic defect. There are no heterozygous phenotypes, Mendelian ratio data, or sample sizes. The models are consistent with the CLSD skeletal/collagen secretion phenotype but not with CHD or CDH.

Strength of model organism evidence: LIMITED (relevant to skeletal disease, not to CHD or CDH)

4. BIOLOGICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Function: SEC23A drives COPII-dependent ER export. Paralog interchangeability and yeast complementation show equivalent protein function, so phenotypic differences reflect expression patterns (PMID: 30065114). Increased SEC23A expression rescues SEC23B-deficient CDAII features (PMID: 34818036).
- Regulation: ULK1 phosphorylates Sec23A at S207, S312 and T405, which inhibits ER-to-Golgi traffic during autophagy (PMID: 28486929). Cysteine mono-ubiquitylation at C432 and C449 modulates COPII formation (PMID: 28553408). CREB3L2/BBF2H7 drives Sec23A/Sec24D expression in hepatic stellate cell activation (PMID: 28801610). miR-375 and miR-200 family members target the SEC23A 3'UTR in prostate and breast cancer (PMID: 21593139; PMID: 21822286).
- A SEC23A-to-SEC23B switch occurs in neuronal differentiation (PMID: 35273078).
- In patient fibroblasts with M702V, collagen secretion was defective and the ER enlarged, with reduced Erk phosphorylation and cyclin D1 and G0/G1 arrest (ASHG 2010 - A novel SEC23A missense mutation causing Cranio-Lenticulo-Sutural Dysplasia is involved in the MAPK signaling pathway).

None of this evidence concerns heart or diaphragm development.

Strength of biological evidence: MODERATE for general COPII/collagen secretion function; LIMITED for relevance to CHD or CDH.

5. OVERALL ASSESSMENT
Overall evidence level for congenital heart disease (CHD): INSUFFICIENT
Overall evidence level for congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH): INSUFFICIENT
Overall evidence level: INSUFFICIENT

Key strengths: SEC23A has a well-characterized role in collagen/ECM secretion. Zebrafish and mouse-related models support its importance in cartilage and skeletal development, and a human recessive disorder (CLSD) exists.

Key gaps: There are no human patients with heterozygous SEC23A variants and CHD or CDH. No model shows a cardiac or diaphragmatic phenotype, and no heterozygous or Mendelian ratio data exist. The established human disease is recessive and skeletal.

Evidence does not point to a syndrome affecting CHD and CDH. The known SEC23A phenotype (CLSD) is a craniofacial/skeletal and collagen-secretion disorder. SEC23A is not supported as a CHD or CDH gene with heterozygous pathogenic variants based on the available literature.

DLGAP4 LITERATURE EVIDENCE REPORT: EPILEPTIC ENCEPHALOPATHY (HETEROZYGOUS / AUTOSOMAL DOMINANT)

1. GENE SUMMARY
DLGAP4 (SAPAP4) encodes a postsynaptic scaffold protein of the DLGAP/SAPAP family. It is part of the PSD95-SHANK-Homer complex at glutamatergic synapses and organizes synaptic structure and signaling. It also has a role in cortical development, where it acts in the ventricular zone (PMID: 35585091).

2. CLINICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Independent case reports/series: none were captured in the structured clinical evidence (0 entries).
- Total patients: none from the clinical track. Two patient-level observations appear only in the biological evidence:
  - A single patient with a novel DLGAP4 variant and an EML1-like subcortical heterotopia phenotype (PMID: 35585091).
  - A five-generation family with a t(8;20)(p12;q11.23) translocation that truncates DLGAP4 and co-segregates with early-onset non-progressive cerebellar ataxia (PMID: 24986922).
- Phenotypes: no seizure or epileptic encephalopathy phenotype is documented in any of the evidence. The reported human phenotypes are subcortical heterotopia and cerebellar ataxia.
- Variant type: no systematic data. The variant types seen are a single variant of unspecified type (not specified in the evidence provided; the study used a "mutant DLGAP4" that failed to rescue) and a balanced translocation causing truncation with epigenetic dysregulation and monoallelic expression.
- Cohorts (UDN, GREGoR, DDD, RUMC, GEL): none identified.
- Strength of clinical evidence: NONE for epileptic encephalopathy. The human data are very sparse and relate to other phenotypes.

3. MODEL ORGANISM EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Organism: mouse only, covering full knockout, heterozygous knockout and in utero electroporation knockdown.
- Full Sapap4 knockouts are viable. They show reduced prefrontal spine density, altered PSD composition (reduced PSD95, GluR1 and GluR2, increased SHANK3), hyperactivity, impulsivity, memory deficits, decreased prepulse inhibition, a mania-like phenotype, and hyperactivity partly rescued by valproate (PMID: 35368073).
- Knockouts also show cognitive deficits, impaired vocal communication and social interaction, autism-like behavior, and altered synapse morphology, function and plasticity (PMID: 30664629).
- Knockdown in utero causes a ventricular surface phenotype, neuronal migration defects, disrupted radial glial adherens junctions and altered actin dynamics. This is rescued by wild-type but not mutant DLGAP4. Heterozygous knockout mice show dorsal telencephalon developmental defects (PMID: 35585091).
- No spontaneous seizures or epilepsy phenotypes are reported, and Mendelian ratios and sample sizes are not given.
- Consistency with human: the neurodevelopmental, cognitive, social and migration phenotypes are broadly relevant to neurodevelopmental disorders. The heterotopia-like migration defect fits the single human patient. There is no direct concordance with epileptic encephalopathy.
- Strength of model organism evidence: MODERATE for a neurodevelopmental disorder role, LIMITED for epileptic encephalopathy specifically.

4. BIOLOGICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY
- Function and pathways: DLGAP4 is a glutamatergic postsynaptic scaffold. It interacts with EML1 and other ventricular zone proteins and affects adherens junction and actin regulation (PMID: 35585091). It is a validated target of the bipolar disorder-associated miR-1908-5p, together with other glutamatergic synapse genes (PMID: 28035180).
- Expression: it is strongly expressed in the mouse ventricular zone from early corticogenesis (PMID: 35585091). The long brain-specific isoform is expressed in fetal and adult cerebellum, especially Purkinje cells, in mouse and human (ASHG 2010 - A familial translocation as a model for decoding regulatory mechanisms associated with an inherited epimutation).
- Epigenetic dysregulation: the translocation causes promoter hypermethylation and monoallelic dysregulated expression (PMID: 24986922).
- Non-neurodevelopmental findings: circDLGAP4 is reduced in ischemic stroke (PMID: 29114076; PMID: 31933904, 170 patients and 170 controls, AUC 0.816) and in sevoflurane neurotoxicity (PMID: 38000773). DLGAP4 is overexpressed in hepatocellular carcinoma (PMID: 36396671) and gastric cancer (PMID: 30365066). These are not relevant to epileptic encephalopathy.
- Strength of biological evidence: MODERATE for neurodevelopmental and synaptic relevance, LIMITED for epilepsy.

5. OVERALL ASSESSMENT
- Overall evidence level: INSUFFICIENT for DLGAP4 as a heterozygous epileptic encephalopathy gene.
- Strengths: consistent mouse data showing synaptic, cognitive, social and cortical developmental abnormalities (PMID: 35368073; PMID: 30664629; PMID: 35585091). There is a plausible synaptic and neurodevelopmental mechanism, and some human genetic hints from one heterotopia patient and one ataxia family.
- Gaps: no clinical case series, no documented patients with epilepsy or epileptic encephalopathy, no de novo or heterozygous LoF/missense cohort data, and no seizure phenotype in mice. The human observations involve other phenotypes (ataxia, heterotopia) and a translocation or epigenetic mechanism, not clearly a dominant pathogenic variant mechanism.
- Conclusion: DLGAP4 is a biologically plausible neurodevelopmental gene, supported by mouse models, but there is currently no direct human genetic evidence linking heterozygous variants to epileptic encephalopathy. Evidence is insufficient, and the gene would require multiple unrelated patients with seizure phenotypes and pathogenic variants before any gene-disease association could be considered.

## REFERENCES


### Model Organism Evidence
1. Novel role of the synaptic scaffold protein Dlgap4 in ventricular surface integrity and neuronal migration during cortical development (PMID: 35585091)
2. Sapap4 deficiency leads to postsynaptic defects and abnormal behaviors relevant to hyperkinetic neuropsychiatric disorder in mice (PMID: 35368073)
3. Cognitive impairment and autistic-like behaviour in SAPAP4-deficient mice (PMID: 30664629)

### Biological Evidence
1. Epigenetic remodelling and dysregulation of DLGAP4 is linked with early-onset cerebellar ataxia (PMID: 24986922)
2. Bipolar Disorder Associated microRNA, miR-1908-5p, Regulates the Expression of Genes Functioning in Neuronal Glutamatergic Synapses (PMID: 28035180)
3. Circular RNA DLGAP4 Ameliorates Ischemic Stroke Outcomes by Targeting miR-143 to Regulate Endothelial-Mesenchymal Transition Associated with Blood-Brain Barrier Integrity (PMID: 29114076)
4. Circular RNA DLGAP4 is down-regulated and negatively correlates with severity, inflammatory cytokine expression and pro-inflammatory gene miR-143 expression in acute ischemic stroke patients (PMID: 31933904)
5. Circular RNA DLGAP4 alleviates sevoflurane-induced neurotoxicity by regulating miR-9-5p/Sirt1/BDNF pathway (PMID: 38000773)
6. DLGAP4 acts as an effective prognostic predictor for hepatocellular carcinoma and is closely related to tumour progression (PMID: 36396671)
7. Examination of the expression and prognostic significance of DLGAPs in gastric cancer using the TCGA database and bioinformatic analysis (PMID: 30365066)

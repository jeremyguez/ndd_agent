# LITERATURE EVIDENCE REPORT: KIAA0100 AS A NEURODEVELOPMENTAL DISORDER GENE

## 1. GENE SUMMARY

KIAA0100, also known as BLTP2 (bridge-like lipid transfer protein 2), is a large multidomain protein that functions as a lipid transfer protein mediating bulk lipid transport between the endoplasmic reticulum (ER) and plasma membrane (PM). The protein localizes to ER-PM contact sites through interactions with phosphoinositides and adaptor proteins, playing a critical role in plasma membrane dynamics and cellular homeostasis (PMID: 40899996, PMID: 39974967).

## 4. BIOLOGICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY

**Known Pathways and Molecular Function:**

KIAA0100/BLTP2 functions as a bridge-like lipid transfer protein that tethers the ER to tubular endosomes and macropinosomes, mediating bulk lipid transport between the ER and PM (PMID: 40899996). The protein localizes to ER-PM contacts through multiple protein-protein interactions with phosphoinositides, adaptor proteins FAM102A/FAM102B, and N-BAR domain proteins (PMID: 39974967). Absence of BLTP2 results in accumulation of intracellular vacuoles connected to the PM, indicating disrupted membrane dynamics (PMID: 40899996).

**Expression Pattern and Structure:**

Bioinformatic analysis predicts KIAA0100 contains a signal peptide, transmembrane region, and four FMP27 domains, suggesting localization to the secretory pathway (PMID: 29404013). Northern blot analysis in U937 cells revealed two mRNA products (6000-10000 bp and 5000-6000 bp) with corresponding protein products (254 kDa and <250 kDa), indicating alternative splicing or post-translational modification (PMID: 29404037). However, no specific data on brain expression or neurodevelopmental tissue distribution is available.

**Key Experimental Findings:**

In cell culture studies, KIAA0100 silencing by siRNA in MDA-MB-231 breast cancer cells reduced cell aggregation, reattachment, metastasis, and invasion, and sensitized cells to anoikis; the protein stabilizes microtubule structure and interacts with heat shock protein HSPA1A (PMID: 29867023). CRISPR/Cas9-mediated downregulation of KIAA0100 in U937 monocytic leukemia cells inhibited proliferation and promoted apoptosis, suggesting an anti-apoptotic function (PMID: 29404013). In lung cancer, KIAA0100 is a target of miR-512-5p and upregulation promotes malignant behaviors including proliferation, migration, invasion, and angiogenesis (PMID: 35866672).

**Cancer-Related Expression:**

BCOX1 (homologous to KIAA0100) is overexpressed in breast carcinoma, with expression absent in benign ductal epithelial cells but elevated in invasive and metastatic carcinoma (PMID: 16289875). Whole-exome sequencing of 146 breast tumors from Hispanic/Latina women identified a recurrent copy-number amplification at 17q11.2 associated with high KIAA0100 expression and breast cancer aggressiveness (PMID: 37145128). KIAA0100 was identified as one of 11 key prognostic genes in a Cox proportional risk model for lung squamous cell carcinoma survival prediction (PMID: 39724502).

**Strength of Biological Evidence: MODERATE**

The biological evidence demonstrates clear molecular function in membrane dynamics and lipid transport, with well-characterized protein interactions and cellular roles. However, the evidence is primarily derived from cancer biology and cell culture studies rather than neurodevelopmental contexts. No data specifically address KIAA0100 function in neuronal development, synaptogenesis, or neurodevelopmental pathways.

## 5. OVERALL ASSESSMENT

**Overall Evidence Level: INSUFFICIENT**

**Key Strengths:**
- Well-characterized molecular function in ER-PM lipid transport and membrane dynamics
- Multiple independent studies confirming protein interactions and cellular localization
- Evidence of functional importance in cellular processes (proliferation, apoptosis, migration)

**Key Gaps and Limitations:**
- Complete absence of clinical evidence: no case reports, case series, or patient cohort data linking KIAA0100 variants to any human disease
- No model organism studies examining in vivo function or neurodevelopmental phenotypes
- No evidence of brain-specific expression or neurodevelopmental pathway involvement
- All functional data derived from cancer biology and cell culture; no neurodevelopmental context
- No variant data (frequency, segregation, functional impact) in neurodevelopmental disorder cohorts
- No assessment of whether heterozygous variants would be pathogenic

**Conclusion:**

While KIAA0100/BLTP2 is a well-characterized protein with important cellular functions in membrane dynamics and lipid transport, there is currently insufficient evidence to support its role as a neurodevelopmental disorder gene. The complete absence of clinical cases, model organism studies, and neurodevelopmental-specific functional data precludes classification as a credible neurodevelopmental disorder gene at this time. To establish such a role, clinical case reports with functional validation, model organism studies demonstrating neurodevelopmental phenotypes, and evidence of brain-specific expression would be required.

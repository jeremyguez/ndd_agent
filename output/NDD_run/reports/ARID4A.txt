# LITERATURE EVIDENCE REPORT: ARID4A AS A NEURODEVELOPMENTAL DISORDER GENE

## 1. GENE SUMMARY

ARID4A (AT-Rich Interaction Domain 4A) is a chromatin remodeling protein containing an N-terminal interdigitated double Tudor domain with DNA-binding activity. It functions as a transcriptional coactivator in epigenetic complexes regulating gene expression, genomic imprinting, and histone modifications, with roles in both hematopoietic and neurological development.

## 2. CLINICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY

**Number of independent case reports/series:** 4 distinct clinical studies identified

**Total approximate number of patients:** 9 patients with neurodevelopmental phenotypes
- 1 patient with schizophrenia (PMID: 35365808)
- 1 patient with MCA-ID and neuroblastoma (PMID: 24665034)
- 6 patients with neurodevelopmental disorders from exome sequencing cohort (ASHG 2017)
- 1 patient with ring chromosome 14 (ASHG 2014)

**Most common phenotypes observed:**
- Developmental delay (present in 7/9 patients)
- Intellectual disability (6/9 patients)
- Behavioral abnormalities and autism (5/9 patients)
- Brain anomalies including corpus callosum abnormalities (5/9 patients)
- Tone abnormalities (4/9 patients)
- Developmental regression (3/9 patients)
- Seizures/epilepsy (2/9 patients)

**Predominant variant type:** Mixed—1 frameshift (loss-of-function), 5 missense variants, and 2 structural variants (microdeletion and ring chromosome). The frameshift and missense variants were identified as de novo in the ASHG 2017 cohort, while the schizophrenia-associated missense variant (p.His411Asp) was heterozygous in affected siblings.

**Cohort source:** The largest case series (6 patients) came from a multi-center US clinical exome sequencing study of 11,649 probands with neurodevelopmental disorders (ASHG 2017). Other cases were from Han Chinese families and individual case reports; no patients were explicitly from established research cohorts (UDN, GREGoR, DDD, RUMC, GEL).

**Variant pathogenicity support:**
- Missense variants showed damaging in-silico predictions and occurred in evolutionarily conserved amino acids
- The schizophrenia-associated p.His411Asp variant demonstrated functional impairment with weaker dsDNA-binding ability and altered cell cycle progression (PMID: 35365808)
- De novo status in 6/6 ASHG 2017 cases supports pathogenicity
- Haploinsufficiency mechanism proposed based on microdeletion case (PMID: 24665034)

**Strength of clinical evidence: MODERATE**

The evidence includes multiple independent case reports with de novo variants and functional validation in one case, but the total patient number is small (9 patients), and most cases lack detailed functional characterization. The phenotypic spectrum is consistent across cases (developmental delay, intellectual disability, brain anomalies), supporting a coherent clinical entity, but larger cohort studies are needed.

## 3. MODEL ORGANISM EVIDENCE SUMMARY

**Organisms studied:** Mouse (Mus musculus)

**Key phenotypes from knockouts:**

*Arid4a full knockout:*
- Hematopoietic dysfunction: ineffective blood cell production, monocytosis, severe anemia, thrombocytopenia, bone marrow failure, myelofibrosis (PMID: 18728284)
- Splenomegaly and hepatomegaly
- Leukemia progression: 5 of 42 Arid4a(-/-) mice progressed to acute myeloid leukemia (AML); 10 of 12 Arid4a(-/-)Arid4b(+/-) compound knockout mice developed AML or CMML-like myelodysplastic/myeloproliferative disorder
- Altered histone trimethylation: H3K4 (32-fold increase), H3K9 (45-fold increase), H4K20 (2.2-fold increase)
- Decreased Hox gene expression

*Arid4a(-/-)Arid4b(+/-) compound knockout:*
- Progressive loss of male fertility with hypogonadism (PMID: 23487765)
- Seminal vesicle agenesis/hypodysplasia
- Spermatogenic arrest at meiotic and postmeiotic stages
- Impaired blood-testis barrier

*Neurological phenotypes:*
- Arid4a downregulation in TDP-43Q331K mice was associated with improved behavioral phenotype and reduced cognitive dysfunction (PMID: 29556029)

**Viability and Mendelian ratios:** Arid4a(-/-) mice are viable, indicating the gene is not essential for embryonic development. No Mendelian ratio deviations reported.

**Consistency with human phenotype:** The mouse knockout data show that ARID4A loss causes developmental and functional abnormalities, consistent with the neurodevelopmental phenotypes observed in humans. However, the most prominent mouse phenotypes involve hematopoiesis and male fertility rather than primary neurological dysfunction. The TDP-43 study suggesting ARID4A downregulation improves behavior is somewhat inconsistent with human pathogenic variants causing neurodevelopmental disease, though this may reflect context-dependent effects.

**Strength of model organism evidence: MODERATE**

Mouse knockouts demonstrate that ARID4A loss causes significant developmental and functional abnormalities, supporting a role in development. However, the primary phenotypes in mice are hematopoietic and reproductive rather than neurodevelopmental, and the TDP-43 study suggests ARID4A downregulation may be beneficial in some contexts. The lack of specific neurodevelopmental phenotypes in standard Arid4a knockouts limits direct phenotypic correlation with human neurodevelopmental disorders.

## 4. BIOLOGICAL EVIDENCE SUMMARY

**Known pathways and molecular function:**

ARID4A participates in multiple epigenetic regulatory pathways:

1. **Chromatin remodeling and histone modification:** ARID4A is a component of epigenetic complexes with SET1/MLL family members and recruits histone deacetylases (HDACs) through RB1 binding. It regulates histone H3K4, H3K9, and H4K20 trimethylation (PMID: 18728284, PMID: 17043311).

2. **Genomic imprinting:** ARID4A and ARID4B regulate the Prader-Willi/Angelman syndrome imprinting domain through alterations in histone and DNA methylation (PMID: 17043311).

3. **Transcriptional regulation:** ARID4A functions as a transcriptional coactivator for androgen receptor (AR) and retinoblastoma (RB) pathways, physically interacting with RB protein and ARID4B (PMID: 23487765).

4. **RNA-binding and mRNA stabilization:** ARID4A functions as an RNA-binding protein that stabilizes metastasis-suppressing transcripts (MTSS1, TIMP2, RB1, PTEN) through binding to conserved structural elements in their 3'UTRs; the ARID domain is required for this activity (PMID: 40066676).

5. **Nucleosome binding:** ARID4A was identified as a novel nucleosome-binding protein, indicating direct chromatin interaction capacity (PMID: 40794873).

**Expression pattern and brain relevance:**

While specific brain expression data are not detailed in the provided evidence, ARID4A's roles in epigenetic regulation, imprinting, and cell cycle control are highly relevant to neurodevelopment. The identification of de novo ARID4A variants in individuals with developmental delay, intellectual disability, and brain anomalies (ASHG 2017) indicates expression and functional importance in the developing nervous system.

**Key experimental findings:**

- The schizophrenia-associated p.His411Asp missense variant reduces DNA-binding ability and alters cell cycle progression in HEK293T cells, demonstrating that missense variants can impair ARID4A function (PMID: 35365808).
- ARID4A expression is significantly downregulated in high Gleason score prostate cancers and associated with poor prognosis, indicating that ARID4A loss promotes disease progression in somatic contexts (PMID: 29797600).
- ARID4A promoter polymorphisms affect expression levels in bulls, demonstrating that regulatory variants influence ARID4A expression (PMID: 30195942).
- ARID4A contains an N-terminal Tudor domain with stronger DNA-binding affinity than its homolog ARID4B despite 80% sequence identity, suggesting structure-function relationships critical for its role (PMID: 33675746).

**Strength of biological evidence: STRONG**

The biological evidence is comprehensive, demonstrating ARID4A's roles in multiple epigenetic pathways critical for development, its DNA-binding and RNA-binding activities, and functional consequences of variants. The evidence clearly establishes mechanisms by which ARID4A dysfunction could impair neurodevelopment through effects on chromatin remodeling, gene expression, and cell cycle regulation.

## 5. OVERALL ASSESSMENT

**Overall evidence level: MODERATE**

**Key strengths:**

1. **Multiple independent case reports with de novo variants:** Six patients from a large clinical exome sequencing cohort (11,649 probands) with de novo ARID4A variants showing consistent neurodevelopmental phenotypes (developmental delay, intellectual disability, autism, brain anomalies) provide strong evidence for pathogenicity.

2. **Functional validation:** The schizophrenia-associated p.His411Asp variant has been functionally characterized, demonstrating impaired DNA-binding and altered cell cycle regulation (PMID: 35365808).

3. **Comprehensive biological characterization:** ARID4A's roles in chromatin remodeling, epigenetic regulation, genomic imprinting, and nucleosome binding are well-established, providing plausible mechanisms for neurodevelopmental dysfunction.

4. **Structural variants support haploinsufficiency:** A de novo 14q23.1q23.3 microdeletion including ARID4A in a patient with multiple congenital anomalies and developmental delay supports a haploinsufficiency mechanism (PMID: 24665034).

5. **Evolutionary conservation:** Missense variants occur in evolutionarily conserved amino acids, supporting pathogenicity.

**Key gaps and limitations:**

1. **Small patient cohort:** Only 9 total patients with neurodevelopmental phenotypes limits statistical power and phenotypic characterization.

2. **Limited functional studies:** Only one variant (p.His411Asp) has detailed functional characterization; most other variants lack experimental validation.

3. **Inconsistent model organism phenotypes:** Mouse Arid4a knockouts primarily show hematopoietic and reproductive phenotypes rather than primary neurodevelopmental dysfunction, limiting direct phenotypic correlation.

4. **Lack of segregation data:** Most cases are de novo, but segregation studies in families are absent, limiting assessment of incomplete penetrance or variable expressivity.

5. **No established cohort participation:** Cases are not from major research cohorts (UDN, GREGoR, DDD), limiting standardized phenotyping and data sharing.

6. **Mechanistic uncertainty in neurodevelopment:** While ARID4A's epigenetic roles are clear, the specific mechanisms by which ARID4A variants cause neurodevelopmental dysfunction remain incompletely understood.

**Conclusion:**

ARID4A is emerging as a neurodevelopmental disorder gene with evidence from multiple de novo variants in individuals with developmental delay, intellectual disability, autism, and brain anomalies. The biological evidence strongly supports ARID4A's role in epigenetic regulation critical for development, and functional studies demonstrate that missense variants can impair DNA-binding activity. However, the moderate overall evidence level reflects the small patient cohort, limited functional characterization of most variants, and incomplete understanding of neurodevelopmental mechanisms. Larger cohort studies, additional functional validation of variants, and investigation of ARID4A expression and function in developing neural tissue would strengthen the evidence for ARID4A as a neurodevelopmental disorder gene.

## REFERENCES


### Clinical Evidence
1. A novel heterozygous missense variant of the ARID4A gene identified in Han Chinese families with schizophrenia-diagnosed siblings that interferes with DNA-binding activity (PMID: 35365808)
2. Multiple congenital anomalies-intellectual disability (MCA-ID) and neuroblastoma in a patient harboring a de novo 14q23.1q23.3 deletion (PMID: 24665034)

### Model Organism Evidence
1. Identification of chromatin remodeling genes Arid4a and Arid4b as leukemia suppressor genes (PMID: 18728284)
2. ARID4A and ARID4B regulate male fertility, a functional link to the AR and RB pathways (PMID: 23487765)
3. TDP-43 gains function due to perturbed autoregulation in a Tardbp knock-in mouse model of ALS-FTD (PMID: 29556029)

### Biological Evidence
1. Deficiency of Rbbp1/Arid4a and Rbbp1l1/Arid4b alters epigenetic modifications and suppresses an imprinting defect in the PWS/AS domain (PMID: 17043311)
2. Arid4a Suppresses Breast Tumor Metastasis by Enhancing MTSS1 Expression via mRNA Stability (PMID: 40066676)
3. AlphaFold-guided structural analyses of nucleosome binding proteins (PMID: 40794873)
4. Downregulation of ARID4A and ARID4B promote tumor progression and directly regulated by microRNA-30d in patient with prostate cancer (PMID: 29797600)
5. Functional haplotypes of ARID4A affect promoter activity and semen quality of bulls (PMID: 30195942)
6. Structural basis for the DNA-binding activity of human ARID4B Tudor domain (PMID: 33675746)
